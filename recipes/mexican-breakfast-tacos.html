Mexican Breakfast Tacos

Descriptio

These flavorful breakfast tacos are a quick and satisfying way to start your day. Warm corn tortillas are filled with a hearty combination of spicy chorizo sausage and scrambled eggs, creating a deliciously savory base. A sprinkle of melted Monterey Jack cheese adds a creamy richness, while a spoonful of fresh salsa and a dash of hot pepper sauce provide a zesty kick.

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces chorizo sausage
  • cooking spray
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 8(6 inch) corn tortillas
  • 1 cup Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1 dash hot pepper sauce (e.g. Tabasco), or to taste
