Strawberry Banana Blended Baked Oats

Description

These Strawberry Banana Blended Baked Oats offer a wholesome and delightful twist on traditional oatmeal. Combining the natural sweetness of overripe bananas and fresh strawberries with the hearty texture of rolled oats, this recipe creates a smooth batter that's baked to perfection. The addition of eggs, almond milk, honey, vanilla extract, and a hint of cinnamon enhances the flavor profile, while optional chocolate chips provide a touch of indulgence.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1 overripe banana
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk or milk of choice
  • 2 tablespoons honey or sweetener of choic
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup chopped fresh strawberries
  • 1/4 cup chocolate chips (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C).
  2. Combine oats, banana, eggs, almond milk, honey, baking powder, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt in a high speed blender and blend until smooth. Remove the blade and fold in chopped strawberries.
  3. Divide batter evenly between 4 ramekins and sprinkle chocolate chips on top.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until baked oats are puffed and light golden brown, about 25 minutes.
  5. Let cool for at least 5 minutes and serve warm or at room temperature.
