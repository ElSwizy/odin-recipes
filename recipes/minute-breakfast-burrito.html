Minute Breakfast Burrito

Description

The Minute Breakfast Burrito is a quick and satisfying meal perfect for busy mornings. In just five minutes, you can prepare a warm tortilla filled with fluffy scrambled eggs, zesty salsa, and a slice of melted American cheese. This simple combination delivers a delicious start to your day without the hassle. Ideal for on-the-go breakfasts, this burrito is both convenient and customizable to suit your taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons salsa
  • 1 slice reduced-fat American cheese
  • 1 tortilla

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Spray a cereal bowl with nonstick cooking spray. Crack the eggs into the bowl, add the salsa, and stir.
  3. Microwave on high for 1 minute, stir, and cook for another minute or until the mixture firms up.
  4. Place cheese in center of tortilla and top with egg mixture.
  5. Wrap it all up like a burrito and head for the car!
